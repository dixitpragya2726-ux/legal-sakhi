BHARATIYA NYAYA SANHITA, 2023 (BNS) — with corresponding Indian Penal Code (IPC), 1860 references
Sections relating to criminal force, assault, and modesty offences against women

================================================================
SECTION 74, BNS (= SECTION 354, IPC) — ASSAULT OR USE OF CRIMINAL FORCE TO WOMAN WITH INTENT TO OUTRAGE HER MODESTY
================================================================

Whoever assaults or uses criminal force to any woman, intending to outrage or
knowing it to be likely that he will thereby outrage her modesty, shall be
punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which shall not
be less than one year but which may extend to five years, and shall also be
liable to fine.

This section is commonly understood to cover acts of molestation, groping, and unwanted physical contact.

================================================================
SECTION 75, BNS (= SECTION 354A, IPC) — SEXUAL HARASSMENT
================================================================

(1) A man committing any of the following acts—
(i) physical contact and advances involving unwelcome and explicit sexual
overtures; or
(ii) a demand or request for sexual favours; or
(iii) showing pornography against the will of a woman; or
(iv) making sexually coloured remarks,
shall be guilty of the offence of sexual harassment.

(2) An offence under clause (i), (ii), or (iii) of sub-section (1) is
punishable with rigorous imprisonment up to three years, or fine, or both.

(3) An offence under clause (iv) of sub-section (1) is punishable with
imprisonment of either description up to one year, or fine, or both.

================================================================
SECTION 78, BNS (= SECTION 354D, IPC) — STALKING
================================================================

(1) Any man who—
(i) follows a woman and contacts, or attempts to contact her to foster
personal interaction repeatedly, despite a clear indication of disinterest by
her; or
(ii) monitors her use of the internet, email, or any other form of electronic
communication,
commits the offence of stalking.

Exceptions apply where such conduct was pursued to prevent or detect crime by
a person entrusted with that responsibility by the State, to comply with a
legal requirement, or where the conduct was reasonable and justified in the
circumstances.

(2) First conviction: imprisonment up to three years, and fine. Second or
subsequent conviction: imprisonment up to five years, and fine.

================================================================
SECTION 79, BNS (= SECTION 509, IPC) — WORD, GESTURE OR ACT INTENDED TO INSULT THE MODESTY OF A WOMAN
================================================================

Whoever, intending to insult the modesty of any woman, utters any words,
makes any sound or gesture, or exhibits any object, intending that it be
heard or seen by such woman, or intrudes upon her privacy, shall be punished
with simple imprisonment for a term which may extend to three years, and also
with fine.

================================================================
SECTION 63, BNS (= SECTION 375, IPC) — RAPE (DEFINITION)
================================================================

A man commits rape if he performs sexual intercourse or specified sexual acts
with a woman without her consent, or in situations including: against her
will; without her consent; by obtaining consent through fear, fraud, or
coercion; when she is under 18 years of age (consent is legally irrelevant in
this case); or when she is unable to communicate consent.

Exception: sexual intercourse by a man with his own wife, where the wife is
not under eighteen years of age, is not rape under this section. (Note: this
marital exception does not apply if the spouses are living separately — see
Section 67, BNS, below. This exception remains legally and socially
contested and has been challenged before the Supreme Court.)

================================================================
SECTION 64, BNS (= SECTION 376, IPC) — PUNISHMENT FOR RAPE
================================================================

Rape is punishable with rigorous imprisonment of not less than ten years,
which may extend to imprisonment for life, along with a fine. Aggravated
categories — such as rape of a woman under 16, rape by a person in a
position of authority (police officer, public servant, member of armed
forces), or repeat offences — carry higher minimum sentences, up to life
imprisonment. Where the act results in the victim's death or a persistent
vegetative state, punishment can extend to the death penalty. This offence is
cognizable, non-bailable, and triable exclusively by a Court of Session.

================================================================
SECTION 67, BNS (= SECTION 376B, IPC) — SEXUAL INTERCOURSE BY HUSBAND WITH WIFE LIVING SEPARATELY, WITHOUT HER CONSENT
================================================================

Whoever has sexual intercourse with his own wife, who is living separately
(whether under a decree of judicial separation or otherwise), without her
consent, shall be punished with imprisonment of two to seven years, and
shall also be liable to fine.

================================================================
SECTION 76, BNS (= SECTION 354B, IPC) — ASSAULT OR USE OF CRIMINAL FORCE WITH INTENT TO DISROBE A WOMAN
================================================================

Any man who assaults or uses criminal force on a woman, or abets such an
act, with the intention of disrobing her or compelling her to be naked, is
punishable with imprisonment of not less than three years, which may extend
to seven years, along with a fine.
This section is commonly understood to cover acts of molestation, groping, inappropriate touching, and unwanted physical contact.

================================================================
SECTION 77, BNS (= SECTION 354C, IPC) — VOYEURISM
================================================================

Any man who watches or captures the image of a woman engaging in a private
act in circumstances where she would usually expect not to be observed —
without her consent — commits the offence of voyeurism. First conviction:
imprisonment of one to three years, and fine. Second or subsequent
conviction: imprisonment of three to seven years, and fine.
