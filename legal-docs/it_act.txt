THE INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY ACT, 2000

================================================================
SECTION 66 — COMPUTER RELATED OFFENCES
================================================================

If any person, dishonestly or fraudulently, does any act referred to in
Section 43 (unauthorized access to a computer system), he shall be punishable
with imprisonment up to three years, or fine up to five lakh rupees, or both.

(The terms "dishonestly" and "fraudulently" carry the meanings assigned to
them under Sections 24 and 25 of the Indian Penal Code, 1860 respectively.)

================================================================
SECTION 66E — PUNISHMENT FOR VIOLATION OF PRIVACY
================================================================

Whoever, intentionally or knowingly captures, publishes, or transmits the
image of a private area of any person without their consent, under
circumstances violating that person's privacy, shall be punished with
imprisonment up to three years, or fine up to two lakh rupees, or both.

Key definitions:
- "transmit" — electronically sending a visual image intending it be viewed
  by a person or persons.
- "capture" — to videotape, photograph, film, or record by any means.
- "private area" — naked or undergarment-clad genitals, pubic area, buttocks,
  or female breast.
- "publishes" — reproduction in printed or electronic form, made available to
  the public.
- "under circumstances violating privacy" — where a person could reasonably
  expect they could disrobe in privacy without their private area being
  captured, or that their private area would not be visible to the public
  regardless of the setting.

================================================================
SECTION 66C — PUNISHMENT FOR IDENTITY THEFT
================================================================

Whoever, fraudulently or dishonestly uses another person's electronic
signature, password, or any other unique identification feature, shall be
punished with imprisonment up to three years, and fine up to one lakh
rupees. This applies, for example, when someone hacks or uses another
person's stolen login credentials to send messages or access accounts under
their identity.

================================================================
SECTION 66D — PUNISHMENT FOR CHEATING BY PERSONATION USING COMPUTER RESOURCE
================================================================

Whoever, by means of any communication device or computer resource, cheats
by personation (pretending to be someone else online to deceive or gain
something dishonestly), shall be punished with imprisonment up to three
years, and fine up to one lakh rupees. This covers phishing, fake profiles
used to defraud someone, and online impersonation scams. Sections 66C and
66D are often invoked together when identity theft leads to an impersonation-
based cheating act.

================================================================
SECTION 67 — PUNISHMENT FOR PUBLISHING OR TRANSMITTING OBSCENE MATERIAL IN ELECTRONIC FORM
================================================================

Whoever publishes or transmits, or causes to be published or transmitted, in
electronic form, any material that is lascivious, appeals to the prurient
interest, or tends to deprave and corrupt persons likely to read, see, or
hear it, shall be punished:
- On first conviction: imprisonment up to three years, and fine up to five
  lakh rupees.
- On second or subsequent conviction: imprisonment up to five years, and fine
  up to ten lakh rupees.
